NEED TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

How do I find a futsal ground?

Use the search to narrow grounds by location, price, and indoor or outdoor court type. Sign in to see venue details and reviews.

How do I book a court?

Choose a ground, date, and available time slot from your player dashboard, then submit a booking request for the venue to confirm.

Can I see my booking status?

Yes. Your player dashboard shows booking updates and notifications after you sign in.
